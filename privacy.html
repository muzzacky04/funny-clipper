Key Privacy Facts

  • Local Architecture: Funny Clipper is primarily configured as a local Windows application. There is no centralized user registration database or hosted tracking backend maintained by us.
  • TikTok OAuth Scope: We request the video.upload scope strictly to transmit your selected clips to your TikTok account as private inbox drafts.
  • No Direct Public Publishing: Uploads land in your TikTok inbox. You maintain complete editorial discretion to publish or discard drafts within the official TikTok application.
  • Local Token Responsibility: OAuth tokens and configuration secrets are retained locally on your computer. You must protect these files and avoid sharing them.
  • Straightforward Revocation: You can revoke TikTok access at any time via your TikTok mobile app security settings and delete local credentials from your computer.

1. Introduction #

This Privacy Policy explains how Funny Clipper (“Application,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by [OWNER NAME], collects, processes, and protects information when you use our video clipping tool and its integrations, including the TikTok Developer integration.

We take creator privacy and platform transparency seriously. We adhere to the principle of data minimization: we only process information that is technically required to analyze video clips, generate subtitles, and upload drafts to your connected TikTok inbox upon your explicit instruction.

2. Information We Collect #

Funny Clipper collects and processes only the minimum data required for its functionality:

  • Video Content & Metadata: Video files, audio streams, file paths, and timestamps selected or supplied by you for clipping and transcription.
  • Third-Party Authentication Data: OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiration timestamps, and authorized scope identifiers returned by TikTok during the authorization flow.
  • Application Configuration Settings: Local user preferences, clipping duration thresholds, subtitle formatting options, and local directory configurations.
  • Operational Logs: Local runtime and diagnostic logs generated on your machine to troubleshoot clipping, audio extraction, or API upload errors.

Funny Clipper does not collect user passwords, credit card details, government identification numbers, or unauthorized profile data.

3. Information Processed Through TikTok Login Kit #

When you choose to connect Funny Clipper to TikTok, the authentication process is handled directly through TikTok’s official OAuth 2.0 Login Kit.

You are redirected to TikTok’s official authorization dialog in your web browser. Funny Clipper never sees, collects, or stores your TikTok password or login credentials. Upon granting permission, TikTok returns an authorization code which Funny Clipper exchanges for access and refresh tokens.

4. TikTok Permissions and Scopes #

Funny Clipper strictly limits requested scopes to what is technically necessary to perform its advertised function:

  • video.upload: This permission enables Funny Clipper to upload your selected video clip to your TikTok account as an inbox draft. This scope does not provide Funny Clipper with access to your private direct messages, your payment details, or the ability to publish publicly without your review.

We do not request scopes to read your follower lists, private messages, user interaction feeds, or modify unrelated account settings.

5. How We Use Information #

Information processed by Funny Clipper is used exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Analyzing designated source videos to identify interesting highlights and segment boundaries.
  • Transcribing speech and rendering synchronized subtitle overlays.
  • Formatting, cutting, and exporting vertical short-form video clips.
  • Authenticating API requests made to TikTok on your behalf using valid OAuth tokens.
  • Initiating draft uploads to your TikTok inbox via the Content Posting API.
  • Displaying local error diagnostics and upload status within the application interface.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or use your data or video content for marketing, advertising profiling, or behavioral targeting.

6. Video and Media Processing #

Funny Clipper processes video media to generate short clips and synchronized subtitles. Regarding the processing architecture:

  • Video clipping, cutting, and rendering operations execute primarily using video processing tools (such as FFmpeg) configured on your local Windows system.
  • Depending on the transcription or detection features configured within your specific environment, subtitle transcription or highlight detection may rely on local speech models or external transcription services/APIs. Where external APIs or speech services are utilized, only the audio or video segments necessary to perform transcription are submitted.
  • Users should review their specific application configuration and external API keys to confirm whether optional third-party speech services are active.

7. TikTok Content Posting API #

Funny Clipper uses the official TikTok Content Posting API under the video.upload scope.

Inbox Draft Destination: Videos transferred via the Content Posting API are deposited strictly into your authenticated TikTok account’s inbox as drafts. They are not posted publicly to your followers or the For You feed by Funny Clipper.

Once a draft is uploaded, the publishing process is completely under your manual control inside the official TikTok mobile app. You may inspect the video, apply TikTok native audio, adjust caption text, set privacy parameters (e.g., Public, Friends, Only Me), or choose to delete the draft.

8. Local Credential and Token Storage #

Funny Clipper operates as a local Windows application. Authentication tokens received from TikTok (access tokens and refresh tokens) and local configuration parameters are stored on your local operating system drive.

User Responsibility for Local Security: Local credential files are stored in plain or system-level configuration files on your workstation. We do not represent or guarantee that credentials are encrypted beyond the protections provided by your local Windows filesystem and user account permissions.

You must safeguard your workstation, apply operating system updates, maintain password protection on your Windows user account, and refrain from sharing your local project directory or credential files with untrusted parties.

9. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services #

We do not share your personal data with third-party data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Data is transmitted only to the third-party platforms you explicitly choose to connect:

  • TikTok (ByteDance Ltd.): When you authenticate and upload clips, video payloads and OAuth tokens are transmitted directly to TikTok endpoints. TikTok processes this data in accordance with the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube (Google LLC): If you import source media from YouTube, your interaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Funny Clipper’s developer does not operate an intermediary server to intercept, copy, or retain your uploaded video files.

10. Data Retention #

Because Funny Clipper functions locally:

  • OAuth Tokens: Retained locally on your machine until they expire, until you refresh them, or until you manually remove the credential files.
  • Generated Video Clips & Subtitles: Stored in your local output folder until you choose to delete them.
  • TikTok Drafts: Governed by TikTok’s internal retention policies once transmitted to your TikTok inbox.

11. Data Deletion and Revocation of Access #

You maintain full sovereignty over your data and platform connections. Funny Clipper does not require an automated web deletion portal because data is stored locally under your direct custody.

How to Revoke TikTok Authorization:

  1. Open the official TikTok application on your mobile device.
  2. Navigate to Profile → tap the menu icon (≡) in the top-right corner.
  3. Select Settings and Privacy.
  4. Go to Security & Permissions (or Security).
  5. Tap Manage App Permissions.
  6. Locate Funny Clipper in the authorized apps list and tap Remove Access (Revoke).

How to Delete Local Data & Credentials:

To remove all traces of Funny Clipper authentication and media from your Windows device:

  1. Delete the local token storage file (e.g., token.json, credentials.json, or .env) containing your OAuth access tokens.
  2. Delete any generated video clips, subtitle files, and temporary cache folders created in your output directories.
  3. Uninstall or delete the Funny Clipper application directory.

For privacy inquiries or assistance regarding data deletion, you may email us directly at [CONTACT EMAIL].

12. Security Measures #

We implement reasonable measures to support software integrity:

  • API communications with TikTok servers are conducted strictly over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.
  • The Application does not transmit credentials to any third-party telemetry server.
  • We do not claim specialized security certifications (such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2) or proprietary hardware-level encryption guarantees. Users are responsible for securing their host operating system.

13. International Data Transfers #

When you upload drafts through the TikTok Content Posting API, your video data and authentication tokens are transferred to servers operated by TikTok and its global affiliates, which may be located in the United States, Singapore, or other jurisdictions.

Please review TikTok’s Privacy Policy to understand their international data transfer safeguards.
