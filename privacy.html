Key Privacy Facts

  • Local Architecture: Funny Clipper is a local Windows application driven by a local n8n workflow pipeline. Core video clipping, speech transcription (Faster Whisper), and AI analysis (Ollama) execute locally on your workstation. There is no centralized SaaS server collecting your source videos.
  • TikTok Integration: We request the video.upload scope strictly to transmit your selected clips to your TikTok account as private inbox drafts. We do not possess or use public publishing permissions.
  • YouTube Integration: We use the YouTube Data API v3 under the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload scope to upload user-selected clips with private visibility. Google API user data is used solely for the requested video upload functionality in compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Review: No automatic public publishing occurs on either TikTok or YouTube. You retain complete editorial control to review, edit, or delete uploads inside the TikTok app or YouTube Studio.
  • Local Token Custody: OAuth credentials are stored in your local secrets/ directory. You are responsible for safeguarding your workstation and credentials.

1. Introduction #

This Privacy Policy explains how Funny Clipper (“Application,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by Moh Fahmi Muzaki, processes and protects information when you use our video clipping application and its third-party platform integrations, including the TikTok Developer integration and the YouTube Data API v3 integration.

Funny Clipper is architected on data minimization principles. Because Funny Clipper operates primarily as a local desktop tool on your Windows computer, processing of your video files and transcription occurs on your local hardware. External network communication takes place only when you initiate third-party platform authentication or request clip staging to TikTok or YouTube.

2. Information We Process #

Funny Clipper processes only the data necessary to execute video analysis, render clips, and fulfill your upload commands:

  • Video & Audio Files: Video media files, audio tracks, and segment timecodes selected or supplied by you for clipping, analysis, and transcription.
  • Source Video Metadata: Video titles, durations, video resolutions, and chapter/transcript timestamps required to generate highlight candidates.
  • Local Processing Files: Temporary audio slices, extracted transcription text, subtitle timing files, and rendered clip files stored in local working folders on your workstation.
  • OAuth Access and Refresh Tokens: Authentication tokens returned by TikTok and Google during the OAuth 2.0 authorization process, stored locally in the secrets/ folder.
  • Application Configuration Settings: Local configuration files (such as pipeline/config.json) containing user preferences, subtitle font selections, language parameters, and clipping thresholds.
  • Local n8n Operational Logs: Workflow execution logs produced on your local workstation to track pipeline progress, debug FFmpeg commands, and diagnose API error codes.
  • Platform Upload Metadata: Clip titles, video descriptions, category identifiers, and publication status information submitted alongside video payloads to TikTok and YouTube APIs.

Funny Clipper does not operate a centralized cloud database, does not collect user account passwords, and does not harvest credit card details or biometric identification.

3. Information Processed Through TikTok Login Kit #

When you connect Funny Clipper to TikTok, authentication is conducted securely via TikTok’s official OAuth 2.0 Login Kit:

  • You are directed to TikTok’s official authorization prompt in your default web browser.
  • Funny Clipper never requests, observes, or stores your TikTok username password.
  • Upon authorization, TikTok returns an authorization code via a local loopback server (e.g., http://localhost:5555/callback/). Funny Clipper exchanges this code directly with TikTok endpoints for an access token and refresh token.

4. TikTok Permissions and Scopes #

Funny Clipper limits TikTok permissions strictly to the operational minimum:

  • video.upload: This permission enables Funny Clipper to upload your selected rendered video clip directly to your authenticated TikTok account’s inbox as a draft.
  • user.info.basic: Used to verify account identification and confirm successful authorization during the OAuth flow.

No Automatic Public Publishing: The video.upload scope allows Funny Clipper to stage drafts into your TikTok inbox only. It does NOT permit automatic public publishing to your followers or feed. Funny Clipper does not request or use an active video.publish scope.

5. How We Use Information #

Information processed by Funny Clipper is used solely for the following functional purposes:

  • Executing local speech-to-text transcription of your video audio.
  • Analyzing transcript text locally using artificial intelligence to identify entertaining highlight moments.
  • Slicing video files, formatting vertical aspect ratios, and burning subtitles via local FFmpeg.
  • Authenticating API requests made to TikTok and YouTube on your behalf.
  • Transmitting selected clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts via the Content Posting API.
  • Transmitting selected clips to your YouTube channel as private videos via the YouTube Data API v3.
  • Logging operational status locally for pipeline monitoring and error troubleshooting.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or utilize your video content or API credentials for advertising profiling, commercial tracking, or machine learning training on third-party servers.

6. Video and Media Processing #

Funny Clipper is engineered as a local-first application executing on your Windows workstation:

  • FFmpeg: Runs locally on your machine for audio extraction, video cutting, scaling, and subtitle burning.
  • Faster Whisper: Runs locally on your workstation to transcribe speech without transmitting raw audio to cloud transcription services.
  • Ollama: Runs locally on your device to analyze transcripts and identify comedic or engaging segments. Local AI processing does not upload your content to external cloud AI vendors.
  • n8n: Operates locally on your machine to orchestrate the step-by-step pipeline.

External Data Transmission: While media processing and AI analysis occur locally, Funny Clipper transmits data externally across the network when you explicitly invoke platform publishing features (transmitting OAuth tokens and video files to TikTok or Google/YouTube endpoints).

7. TikTok Content Posting API #

Funny Clipper uses the official TikTok Content Posting API under the video.upload scope.

When you select a clip for TikTok upload, the video binary and associated metadata are transferred directly to TikTok servers. The video is deposited into your authenticated TikTok account’s inbox as a private draft. It is not published publicly.

You must open the official TikTok application on your mobile device to inspect the draft, adjust sound, captions, or privacy settings, and manually complete publication. Once delivered to TikTok, subsequent processing and storage are governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

8. YouTube API Services and Google User Data Policy #

Funny Clipper integrates with YouTube API Services to allow creators to upload clips to their YouTube channels. By using YouTube publishing features, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • API & Scopes: Funny Clipper utilizes the official YouTube Data API v3 and requests authorization under the scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload.
  • Private Visibility: All videos uploaded to YouTube via Funny Clipper are created with private visibility (privacyStatus: "private"). Funny Clipper does not publish videos publicly.
  • YouTube Studio Review: Uploaded videos remain private on your YouTube channel. You can inspect, edit metadata, change visibility, or delete uploaded videos at any time via YouTube Studio.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance: Funny Clipper’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Limited Use Affirmation: Access to Google user data (OAuth tokens and video upload capabilities) is strictly limited to providing and improving user-facing video uploading functionality requested by the user. Funny Clipper does not use Google user data for serving advertisements, user profiling, surveillance, or resale to third parties.
  • Third-Party Policies: For more details regarding Google’s data handling practices, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

9. Local Credential and Token Storage #

Funny Clipper stores OAuth tokens and configuration secrets on your local Windows computer within the application’s secrets/ directory:

  • YouTube Token File: secrets/youtube_token.json (containing Google OAuth refresh token and temporary access token).
  • TikTok Token File: secrets/tiktok_token.json (containing TikTok OAuth refresh token and temporary access token).

Workstation Security Notice: Credential files are stored on your local workstation filesystem. We make no representations that credentials are encrypted beyond the protections provided by your local Windows operating system account permissions. You are solely responsible for securing your computer, keeping software updated, and ensuring credential files are never shared or committed to public version control.

10. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services #

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third-party data brokers, marketers, or analytics companies.

External data transmission is limited strictly to the third-party platforms you choose to connect:

  • TikTok (ByteDance Ltd.): Video binaries, titles, and OAuth tokens are transmitted to TikTok endpoints when you initiate a TikTok draft upload.
  • Google / YouTube (Google LLC): Video binaries, descriptions, tags, and OAuth tokens are transmitted to Google endpoints when you initiate a YouTube private upload.

The operator of Funny Clipper (Moh Fahmi Muzaki) does not operate an intermediary proxy server or cloud relay to intercept, copy, or retain your video uploads or authentication tokens.

11. Data Retention #

Because Funny Clipper operates locally on your workstation:

  • OAuth Tokens: Retained locally in secrets/ until they expire, are refreshed, or are deleted by you.
  • Media & Transcripts: Source videos, cut clips, subtitle files, and manifests remain in your local project directories until you choose to delete them.
  • Local n8n Logs: Operational execution logs follow your local n8n configuration and disk retention settings.
  • TikTok Drafts: Governed by TikTok’s internal retention policies once uploaded to your TikTok inbox.
  • YouTube Videos: Stored within your YouTube channel account under private status until you publish, modify, or delete them in YouTube Studio.

12. Data Deletion and Revocation of Access #

You maintain full sovereignty over your accounts and credentials. You may revoke access or delete your data at any time without needing an external deletion portal:

How to Revoke TikTok Authorization:

  1. Open the official TikTok application on your mobile device.
  2. Go to Profile → tap the menu icon (≡) → select Settings and Privacy.
  3. Tap Security & Permissions (or Security) → tap Manage App Permissions.
  4. Select Funny Clipper and tap Remove Access.

How to Revoke Google / YouTube Authorization:

  1. Visit your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.
  2. Locate Funny Clipper under third-party apps with account access.
  3. Click Remove Access to revoke OAuth tokens immediately.

How to Delete Local Data & Credentials:

  1. Delete the local credential files: secrets/youtube_token.json and secrets/tiktok_token.json.
  2. Delete the generated media folders (such as clips/, transcripts/, audio/, and analysis/).
  3. Uninstall or delete the application folder from your Windows workstation.

Important Note: Deleting local credentials or revoking platform access does not automatically delete clips already uploaded to TikTok or YouTube. To delete uploaded media, you must delete drafts directly within the TikTok app or delete videos via YouTube Studio.

For privacy inquiries or deletion guidance, contact the operator at mfmzacky4@gmail.com.

13. Security Measures #

We take reasonable technical precautions within the application’s scope:

  • All outbound API calls to TikTok and Google endpoints are executed exclusively over encrypted HTTPS/TLS channels.
  • OAuth authorization uses PKCE (Proof Key for Code Exchange) where supported, and tokens are returned over localhost loopbacks.
  • The Application does not transmit credentials to any external telemetry or third-party tracking server.
  • We do not claim specialized enterprise security certifications (such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2). Users remain responsible for the physical and software security of their Windows workstation.

14. International Data Transfers #

When you initiate draft uploads to TikTok or private uploads to YouTube, your video data and authentication tokens are transmitted to servers operated by ByteDance Ltd. and Google LLC respectively, which may be located in the United States, Singapore, or other countries.

Please review the respective privacy policies of TikTok and Google to learn about their cross-border data transfer safeguards.
