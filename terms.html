2. Description of Funny Clipper #

Funny Clipper is a video clipping automation application designed to help creators identify interesting or entertaining moments from longer video material, generate short-form vertical video clips, render synchronized subtitle overlays, and stage selected clips for social media sharing.

The Application is architected as a local desktop pipeline operating on the user’s Windows workstation, orchestrated through a local n8n workflow. It utilizes local components including FFmpeg for video processing, Faster Whisper for on-device speech transcription, and Ollama for local artificial intelligence analysis. External network communication occurs when you explicitly initiate third-party platform integrations with TikTok or YouTube.
